qPRO — Quick Suprathreshold Profile: Clinician Guide

qPRO integrates a patient's Pure-Tone Audiogram (0.25–8 kHz), qCLS Categorical Loudness Scaling (CU 5–50), and qVCV Consonant-in-Noise Test inside the browser via WebAssembly—no patient data ever leaves your computer.

1. Reading the Six Landscape Panels

  • Panel A (Loudness Scaling & SPL-o-gram): Compares patient loudness contours (CU 5 softest to CU 50 UCL) against Normal-Hearing norms and conversational speech (60 dB SPL). Colored top bands show where Voicing/Nasal, Place-Burst, and Fricative (S/SH/Z) cues lie in frequency.
  • Panel B (Audiogram & Cochlear Loss): Splits pure-tone loss into Outer Hair Cell Loss (loudness recruitment & spectral smearing) vs. Inner Hair Cell / Sensitivity Loss.
  • Panel C (Estimated SNR Loss Breakdown): Separates QuickSIN-style Estimated SNR Loss (dB) into three actionable components: (1) Inaudibility (restorable with gain), (2) Recruitment & Upward Masking (managed via WDRC & low-frequency gain control), and (3) Suprathreshold Clarity Loss (indicating directional or remote-microphone SNR support).
  • Panel D (Expected vs. Actual qVCV Score): Shows whether actual unaided (and aided) recognition matches what the audiogram and qCLS predict—immediately flagging hidden clarity deficits or aided WDRC rollover.
  • Panel E (Consonant Confusion Pattern %): De-noised percentage confusion table plus Speech Cue Clarity (% of Normal) for Voicing, Manner, and Place.
  • Panel F (Quality Checks, REM Targets & Guidance): Automated booth cross-checks, patient-specific 0.5/1/2/4 kHz Comfort (CU 25) and MPO Cap (CU 50) targets in dB SPL for Verifit/REM verification, and targeted fitting bullets.

2. Severity, Smearing & Normative Label Definitions

Important Distinction from ASHA / WHO Pure-Tone Categories: Because audiometric organizations define pure-tone degree-of-loss labels differently (e.g., ASHA uses ≤ 15 dB HL Normal / 16–25 dB Slight / 26–40 dB Mild, whereas WHO uses 4-frequency PTA < 20 dB HL Normal / 20–34 dB Mild), qPRO never uses ambiguous pure-tone severity words for the audiogram itself (reporting exact dB HL values and shading the adult clinical reference zone ≤ 20 dB HL). Instead, the Min / Mild / Mod / Sev and Normal / Mild / Moderate / Severe tags in qPRO have two specific, quantitative suprathreshold definitions:

A. Panel B Bar Tags (Min, Mild, Mod, Sev) — Cochlear Spectral Smearing

Printed at the tips of the bars in Panel B (and summarized in the Panel B title). These indicate auditory filter broadening (Equivalent Rectangular Bandwidth multiplier, ERB = 1 + 1.8 × HLOHC / 50) caused by Outer Hair Cell (OHC) loss—not total pure-tone threshold category:

3. Hearing-Aid Prescription & REM Sheet (qPRO-Rx)

Use the Prescription / REM Sheet toggle in the top toolbar to view and print an individualized Real-Ear Aided Response (REAR) and Insertion Gain prescription across 250–8000 Hz:

  • Loudness-Mapped Targets & Kneepoints (qCLS): Maps Soft (50 dB), Average (65 dB), and Loud (80 dB SPL) ISTS speech bands onto the patient's measured qCLS contours (with audiogram NAL-style fallback if qCLS is unavailable), derives frequency-specific Soft-Speech Compression Kneepoints (CT, dB SPL) near CU 10 (bounded above ambient room noise), and anchors the MPO Cap directly to CU 50.
  • Speech-in-Noise Guardrails: Automatically restrains low-frequency gain (< 1 kHz) when high-frequency recruitment/smearing would cause upward spread of masking, caps high-frequency gain on severe clarity-loss bands (≥ 3 kHz) to avoid distortion, and limits channel WDRC compression ratios to ≤ 2.8 : 1 (with slow release ≥ 400 ms when envelope cue clarity is reduced).
  • Biophysical Outcome Prediction: Simulates the patient's Predicted Aided qVCV (%) under the prescribed gain and WDRC settings so clinicians can verify real-ear aided speech recognition against an individualized expectation.

4. Importing Fixed & Adaptive Web-App Data, Binaural Ears, & Printing

  • Standard Fixed-SNR & Adaptive-SNR qVCV CSVs: When importing an Adaptive qVCV CSV (ADP50 or ADP100), qPRO automatically reads Final_SNR_Loss_dB, tracker SE, and per-consonant presented SNRs, conditioning peripheral cue extraction at the actual test SNRs and mapping recognition onto the apples-to-apples Fixed-SNR Equivalent % scale in Panel D (for both unaided and aided runs).
  • Test + Retest Multi-File Upload: Select two CSV files simultaneously in Unaided qVCV to pool Test and Retest runs, plot Test/Retest markers in Panel D, and run the automated repeatability check in Panel F.
  • Independent Left & Right Ear Slots: Switch Ear (Right (O) ↔ Left (X)) in the toolbar to enter each ear's own audiogram, qCLS, and qVCV; qPRO automatically cross-checks interaural symmetry using each ear's own thresholds and saves profiles in local browser storage.
  • 1-Page Sheet or 2-Page Full Packet PDF: Click Print Sheet for the active view, or Print 2-Page Packet to save both the Diagnostic Profile (Page 1) and Prescription / REM Sheet (Page 2) in a single US Letter Landscape PDF.
